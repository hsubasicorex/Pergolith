Questions worth asking

Before you choose a pergola company.

A careful client should expect specific answers—not vague promises.

How do you determine which pergola model fits my project?+

We consider intended use, dimensions, attachment or freestanding conditions, views, architectural style, comfort options, access and project-specific structural requirements before recommending a system.

Can a pergola make a restaurant patio usable in poor weather?+

A coordinated louvered roof, water management, screens, glass and comfort equipment can significantly extend usability. Exact performance depends on configuration, exposure and operating conditions.

Is a louvered pergola completely waterproof?+

A properly configured bioclimatic system is designed to manage normal rainfall through interlocking louvers, gutters and downspouts. It should not be described as an absolute waterproof room: wind-driven rain, extreme weather, enclosure choices, installation details and maintenance all affect results.

What should happen during high wind, snow or severe weather?+

The owner must follow the operating limits and weather guidance supplied for the specific system. Site exposure, structural requirements, sensors, louver position and snow management are project-specific and should be addressed during planning and handover.

Who handles electrical work, drainage and site preparation?+

The required responsibilities are identified during planning. Pergolith coordinates its installation scope with the owner, contractor and applicable trades so power, drainage and mounting conditions are ready at the right time.

Do I need engineering or permits?+

Requirements vary by municipality, project size, attachment condition and location. We identify known project inputs and coordinate the system information needed for the applicable approval path.

Can screens, glass, lighting or heaters be added?+

Yes, when compatible with the selected system and site. These options should be considered early because they affect dimensions, power, controls, clearances and the overall design.

What happens after installation?+

We provide an operational handover and a service path for maintenance, diagnostics and parts support. Project-specific product and workmanship coverage is documented in the proposal.

How much does a premium pergola project cost?+

Investment depends on dimensions, model, support conditions, finish, screens or glass, lighting, heating, engineering, access and site work. A site-specific proposal is the responsible way to price the complete project.
